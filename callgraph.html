01

The whole program, from the one file you run

main.py does exactly two things: it signs you in, and it looks at your role to decide which menu to hand you to. It contains no business logic at all — every real action lives in one of the six files underneath.

02

The life of one order

An order is one dictionary inside DATA["orders"]. Its status field is the only thing that changes as it travels, and each change is made by exactly one function in exactly one file. This is the picture to memorise.
